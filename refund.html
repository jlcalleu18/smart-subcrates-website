Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

7-day money-back guarantee

If Smart Subcrates is not right for you, email us within 7 days of your purchase and we will refund you in full. You do not need to justify the request.

How to request a refund

Email support@smartsubcrates.com with the email address you used at checkout, and your order number if you have it. The order number is in your receipt from Lemon Squeezy.

What happens next

  • We aim to reply within 2 business days.
  • Refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy, back to your original payment method.
  • Your bank or payment provider usually takes a few business days to show the money.
  • Your licence key is deactivated when the refund is issued.

Before you buy

Most refund requests we see come down to system requirements, so please check first:

  • Smart Subcrates needs macOS 12 Monterey or newer. It will not run on older versions.
  • You need Serato DJ installed to use the crates it creates.
  • There is no Windows version yet.

If you are not sure whether it will work for you, email us before buying and we will tell you honestly.

Problems after 7 days

If something is broken, tell us anyway. The 7-day guarantee is a no-questions-asked window, not the limit of our support. We would rather fix the problem than keep money from someone with an app that does not work.
